**BONKERS!** 🔴🟡
*(2D Platformer Race + Place-Based Sabotage)*

A two-player, one-keyboard platform race where hidden ? boxes hand attacks to whoever is behind and only defence to whoever is ahead, so no lead is ever safe.

**What it is:** Race up six zig-zag rows past lava, spikes, saws and water to reach the flag first. Every ? box has fixed prizes, no luck involved. Chasing? You get attacks like Bonk Fist, Freeze Ray, Gravity Bomb or Swaparoo. Leading? You only get a Shield or a Banana. Every attack can be dodged, and you can shove your rival or stomp on their head. BONK!

**Controls:**
🔴 Red: A / D move · W jump · S use power
🟡 Yellow: ← / → move · ↑ jump · ↓ use power
Space = start / rematch · R = restart · Esc = menu
