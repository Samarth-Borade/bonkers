**Genre:**
2D Platformer, Local Versus Racing

**Twist:**
Place-Based Power-Up Boxes (catch-up without luck)

**Description:**
BONKERS! is a head-to-head platform race: both players climb six zig-zag rows past lava, spikes, rolling saws and water, and the first to touch the flag wins. Most item racers hand out random power-ups, so a lucky pick-up decides the race. In BONKERS! every ? box has fixed prizes, and what you get depends on your place. The player behind gets attacks (Bonk Fist, Freeze Ray, Gravity Bomb, Swap), while the player ahead only gets defence (Shield, Banana). Every attack can be dodged, so the chaser has to aim, the leader has to defend, and races stay neck and neck until the last row.

**Controls:**
Red: A / D move, W jump, S use power-up
Yellow: Left / Right move, Up jump, Down use power-up
Shove: run into the other player
Stomp: land on their head
Start / Rematch: Space
Restart: R
Menu: Esc
